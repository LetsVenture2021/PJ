## Objective

Standardize diligence with evidence, controls, and accountable decisions.

## Scope

Covers intake, analysis, risk, approval, submission, and archive for all opportunities.

## Roles

Deal Lead owns recommendation. Analyst compiles evidence. Finance and Legal review controls. Sponsor approves readiness.

## Prerequisites

Opportunity ID, required source files, assigned reviewers, timeline, evidence register, and risk register.

## Procedure

Triage intake; define diligence plan; collect verified evidence; run financial scenarios; complete legal review; score risks and mitigations; draft thesis and anti thesis; resolve reviewer conflicts; finalize and seal source; export PDF and archive package.

## Exceptions

Expedited flow allowed with sponsor approval, but legal review and risk scoring remain mandatory.

## Revision Notes

Version 1 sets baseline controls, gates, and quality expectations for committee submissions.
