# Comprehensive Investment Diligence SOP

> **Doc:** DOC-65bf45f1 v1 · **Template:** sop · **Status:** FINAL · **Created:** 2026-08-03T05:57:55.497414+00:00

## Objective

Establish a comprehensive, repeatable operating procedure for end-to-end due diligence packet assembly and investment committee readiness so teams can move from intake to decision with traceable quality controls, measurable cycle times, and explicit accountability.

## Scope

This SOP applies to all pre-investment opportunities from initial lead qualification through final committee package submission. It covers data intake, founder and market analysis, financial model review, legal/compliance checks, risk logging, memo drafting, stakeholder approvals, and archival requirements. It applies to analysts, associates, deal leads, legal, finance, and operations across normal, expedited, and exception workflows.

## Roles

Deal Lead: accountable owner for opportunity progression, gating decisions, and final recommendation. Analyst: prepares research pack, source validation table, and first-pass risk register. Finance Reviewer: validates unit economics, runway assumptions, sensitivity tests, and downside scenarios. Legal/Compliance Reviewer: confirms entity documentation completeness and regulatory fit. Operations Coordinator: enforces timeline checkpoints, artifact integrity, version control, and checklist completeness. Investment Committee Sponsor: signs off final submission quality and alignment to mandate.

## Prerequisites

1) Opportunity ID created and linked to CRM record. 2) Minimum intake documents received (pitch deck, cap table snapshot, financial statements or model, incorporation details). 3) Diligence workspace initialized with governed folder structure and naming convention. 4) Source-of-truth index created listing every external and internal input with provenance. 5) Review calendar agreed with owners and hard deadlines for each gate. 6) Known conflicts of interest declaration completed for all reviewers.

## Procedure

Phase 1 — Intake & Triage: Validate submission completeness; score strategic fit, sector alignment, and stage suitability; assign preliminary confidence level. Phase 2 — Structured Diligence: Run market sizing and competition mapping, customer/reference checks, product and technical assessment, and financial model stress tests; document all claims with verifiable evidence links. Phase 3 — Risk & Controls: Populate risk register by category (market, product, team, legal, governance, concentration, execution); classify impact/probability; define mitigations and owners; escalate red flags immediately. Phase 4 — Investment Narrative: Draft committee memo with thesis, anti-thesis, key assumptions, valuation context, scenario outcomes, and decision options; include unresolved questions with closure plan. Phase 5 — Multi-Role Review: Conduct legal, finance, and sponsor reviews in sequence; log required changes; re-run fact and consistency checks after edits; secure explicit approvals. Phase 6 — Finalization & Submission: Freeze approved version, seal artifact integrity metadata, export audience-ready package to PDF, and submit to committee distribution channel before cutoff. Phase 7 — Post-Decision Archive: Record decision outcome, rationale traceability, and lessons learned; archive final package and evidence index for audit retrieval.

## Exceptions

Expedited opportunities may compress timeline but cannot bypass legal/compliance sign-off, risk register population, or evidence-backed recommendation. Missing critical documents triggers hold status until resolved. Any material contradiction between sources requires escalation to Deal Lead and sponsor before submission. If required reviewers are unavailable, backup delegates with equivalent authority must be appointed in writing.

## Revision Notes

Version 1 establishes the baseline lifecycle, control gates, and evidence standards. Future revisions should capture KPI benchmarks (median turnaround, defect rate in committee packs, rework causes), refine risk taxonomy, and codify automation opportunities for intake validation and evidence integrity checks.
