## Objective

Provide a complete standard operating procedure for investment due diligence so every opportunity is reviewed with consistent evidence, clear ownership, and documented decision logic.

## Scope

This procedure covers intake, analysis, risk review, recommendation drafting, approval, submission, and archive. It applies to analysts, finance reviewers, legal reviewers, deal leads, and committee sponsors for all new opportunities.

## Roles

Deal Lead owns final recommendation and gate decisions. Analyst prepares evidence summaries and source logs. Finance Reviewer validates assumptions, sensitivity cases, and valuation context. Legal Reviewer validates governance, compliance, and legal risk. Operations Coordinator tracks deadlines and package completeness. Committee Sponsor approves readiness for submission.

## Prerequisites

Create an opportunity record and unique identifier. Collect required source files including deck, cap table, and financial model. Open a diligence workspace with standard naming rules. Assign reviewers and due dates. Create an evidence register and risk register before analysis begins.

## Procedure

1. Intake and triage: verify required files and strategic fit. 2. Diligence plan: define questions, owners, and deadlines. 3. Evidence collection: gather market, customer, product, and team inputs with source links. 4. Financial analysis: normalize assumptions and run base, downside, and stress scenarios. 5. Legal and compliance review: confirm structure, obligations, and constraints. 6. Risk synthesis: score probability and impact, assign mitigation owners, and record escalation triggers. 7. Recommendation draft: write thesis, anti thesis, assumptions, options, and decision rationale. 8. Cross functional review: resolve conflicts and capture residual uncertainty. 9. Final approval: secure sponsor sign off, freeze the final document, and lock version lineage. 10. Distribution and archive: export audience ready PDF and archive all source evidence for retrieval.

## Exceptions

Expedited review may shorten timeline but cannot skip legal review, risk scoring, or evidence backed recommendation requirements. Missing critical files place the opportunity on hold. High impact conflicts require escalation before submission.

## Revision Notes

Version 1 defines required controls, stage gates, and accountability rules. Later versions should update KPI thresholds and recurring defect patterns while keeping the same governance controls.
